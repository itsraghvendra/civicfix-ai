How CivicFix Works

A four-step civic participation and transparent redressal process for Lucknow.

1

Report a Problem

Spot a public safety, road, garbage, streetlight, or water issue in your neighbourhood and begin by entering a title.

2

Add Details and a Photo

Choose your locality, add landmark notes, and optionally upload photo evidence to help demonstrate the severity.

3

Receive a Tracking ID

Receive an immediate reference ID (CF-2026-XXXX) along with transparent AI category and priority recommendations.

4

Track Status Updates

Follow status updates in real time (Submitted → In Review → In Progress → Resolved) via the public tracking screen.

About CivicFix AI

Empowering communities and municipal staff through intelligent, transparent civic technology.

Our Mission for Lucknow

CIVICFIX AI was conceived to bridge the gap between everyday citizen awareness and municipal responsiveness. By combining accessible, human-centric design with modern artificial intelligence, we enable residents to document civic concerns clearly and transparently.

AI Ethics & Human-in-the-Loop

AI classifications and priority suggestions generated by Groq (or our deterministic municipal fallback) are advisory. Final administrative dispatch, prioritization, and resolution are strictly managed by human municipal officers.

Civic Privacy Standard

We follow strict data minimization: we do not harvest GPS breadcrumbs or residential addresses. Citizen contact details and internal operational logs are segregated from public tracking views.
